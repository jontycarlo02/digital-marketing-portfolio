05 — Case Study / Oryxis
ORYXIS
Time. Perfected.

Oryxis was developed as a modern luxury watch brand built around precision, craftsmanship and understated elegance.

The project explored how a complete brand world could be translated across identity, product presentation, website and email marketing.

BUILT AROUND
PRECISION.
ORYXIS

Modern luxury, built with intention.

The Oryxis identity combines deep black, metallic gold and refined typography to create a visual language rooted in craftsmanship, precision and exclusivity.

The objective was to create a brand that felt premium without becoming excessive — elegant, confident and unmistakably modern.
